0. Engine inputs and optional vehicle selection

The calculation starts with prefilled, editable fields. The initial example is the 1ZR-FAE combustion engine with a bounded 97 kW / 160 N·m envelope. Geometry, pressure and forces remain in the separate 720° module. Selecting a vehicle is optional.

Engine, electric-drive and vehicle parameters are kept separate. Combustion-engine power, torque and speed fields constrain the source envelope; they do not create a new measured characteristic. Raising a limit beyond the source boundary adds no power. A different engine requires its own data.

Selecting a profile or using the advisor first displays the proposed changes. Manual editing protects a field from replacement; that protection can be cleared. Changes are applied only with a separate button. The most recent application can be undone.

Vehicle profiles are author-defined starting assumptions, not production-vehicle specifications. The advisor does not relax requirements to declare a configuration suitable. A missing 1ZR fuel map means unknown consumption, not zero.

1. The powertrain as a system

The top calculation level is the vehicle and its powertrain, rather than an isolated combustion engine. Supported layouts are battery-electric drive (BEV), a P2 parallel hybrid, a series hybrid and a conventional combustion engine for comparison. The existing 720° combustion-engine calculation remains separately available in ice.html; it has not been replaced by an efficiency table.

In P2, the combustion engine and electric machine act on a common shaft upstream of the gearbox. At a given vehicle speed, their torques can be added only when rotational speeds, transmission ratio and battery limits are compatible. Rated maximum powers cannot be added independently of speed. In a series layout, only the traction electric machine is mechanically connected to the wheels; the engine supplies a generator and the DC bus [9].

This is a quasi-steady backward calculation: prescribed v(t), gradient and vehicle parameters determine the required force. Powertrain limits are checked at every step. If traction force or auxiliary demand cannot be supplied, the deficit is retained and the mission is marked incomplete. Prescribed speed is not reported as an actually achieved speed.

The configurations are engineering scenarios, not models of a production Outlander or Toyota Hybrid. The baseline mass of 1950 kg, Cd = 0,33 and A = 2,65 m² are example values taken from the library configuration; the new transmission ratios, battery, controls and electric machines do not reproduce that vehicle. Using the same mass deliberately isolates the effect of architecture; the mass of the actual battery pack and assemblies must be accounted for separately [2,3].

No claim is made of Euro 5/6 type approval, test-bench validation of the complete powertrain or a certified driving range. Having a new engine or a catalogue peak efficiency does not establish its full map, controller calibration or thermal capability.

2. Calibration and limits of the evidence

For the 1ZR-FAE, only a two-point alignment of output torque was performed: Mкор(n) = M720(n)·(a + b·n). The reference conditions are 160 N·m at 4400 min⁻¹ and 97 kW at 6400 min⁻¹, corresponding to 144,732 N·m at the second point. Two coefficients were determined from two points; no independent verification conditions are available. This aligns the output by interpolation; it does not identify combustion processes [1].

Extending the correction without limits to low speeds exceeds the rated maximum torque. Therefore explicit limits of 160 N·m and 97 kW are used for preliminary selection. The portion below 4400 min⁻¹ is an assumed bounded envelope; its plateau is not a measured Toyota characteristic. The uncorrected result and the unbounded correction remain in the calibration artifact.

Pressure, temperature, heat balance, fuel consumption and dynamic loads in the 720° module remain results of the original uncalibrated model. The aligned 1ZR envelope has no known fuel map: the application returns “fuel map unavailable” rather than scaling fuel consumption with torque. This preserves the distinction between a power-selection characteristic and a physical cycle.

The Outlander GG2W is the user's own experimental project with an OBD-II logger (author clarification dated 24.09.2026). According to the source README, the Engine Power channel is calculated by the MX+ application, so it is not test-bench power. In the Willans artifact, the input is named engine_power_kw_obd and the target is fuel_g_s_maf: fuel flow derived from MAF/(14,1·λ), rather than an independent fuel-flow meter. This dataset is suitable for a vehicle-specific empirical model, but error relative to this reference is not the absolute error in physical fuel flow [2].

The GG2W Willans coefficients were copied unchanged from vehicle_model_library_examples_v0.11: ṁf = 0,0700027543 + 0,0655540723·Pe, with ṁf in g/s and Pe in kW. At Hu = 43 MJ/kg, the incremental efficiency implied by the slope is approximately 35,48%. The regression intercept of 0,0700 g/s is not a measured idle fuel flow: an assumed 0,22 g/s is specified separately [2]. The application uses an explicit low-load fuel-flow floor of max(0,22; b0+b1Pe) g/s; it prevents consumption from falling below the assumed idle value and was not calibrated with the library coefficients.

In the supplied artifact, integrated fuel error against the MAF/lambda estimate on the test set is −3,088% for the fuel model alone, but −66,565% for the complete hybrid chain. The latter is not acceptable validation of the vehicle model. The original raw trips are absent from the package used; these metrics are reported by the library, not a validation rerun by us. The controller from that chain was not adopted as a verified controller [2].

For retraining on new observations, fit-willans.mjs accepts CSV columns group_id,power_kw,fuel_g_s,dt_s. Whole groups/trips are separated; every fifth ordered group forms the holdout set. Time-weighted squared error is minimized subject to nonnegative coefficients; at least 5 groups, 20 points and variation in power are required. P < 1 kW, nonnumeric values and invalid data are rejected with a row log. A new fit does not replace the previous one automatically.

The source diagnostics explicitly state that energy reserve, Battery Charge / Save modes and charging cannot be established without correct traction-battery SOC or a mode flag. Excess calculated engine power may include auxiliary loads, warm-up and channel error. Battery Rint and regeneration in this application are separate explicit simplifications, not claimed to be calibrated against these logs [2].

3. Combustion-engine and electric-machine maps

EPA describes constructing full ALPHA maps from experimental and modeled regions, including operating-domain definition, separate low-load conditions and verification. A vehicle model needs fuel flow as a function of speed and torque; BSFC and efficiency are derived quantities, not substitutes for idle fuel flow. Cold-start and transient corrections are outside this steady-state implementation [7].

The official EPA page also provides electric-drive maps. EMOT includes the motor and inverter; EDU additionally includes the transmission; BISG includes the belt drive. An inverter or gearbox must not be counted twice. The current importer accepts motor-only and motor+inverter maps. EDU/BISG maps are rejected until a separate adapter for their mechanical boundary is implemented [8].

EPA numerical ZIP packages were not downloaded for this release: methodology pages and the link catalogue were checked, but not numerical matrices. None of the built-in maps is described as an EPA map. The powertrain-grid/1 importer accepts prepared JSON with rpm, torqueNm and values; rows are speeds and columns are torques. This is our exchange format, not a claimed native ALPHA format.

For fuel_g_s, Hu and maxTorqueNm are required at every speed. For loss_w, the system boundary is mandatory; negative torques have their own cells. Zero is a numeric zero; null is missing data. Bilinear interpolation stays inside the map and does not turn missing cells into zeros. At an exact grid node, neighboring cells with zero weight have no effect.

For the EMRAX 228, 28 points were digitized from the 94% and 95% efficiency contours on page 1 of the supplied datasheet. Nonnegative coefficients were fitted in loss space: L = c0 + c1ν + c2ν² + c3ν³ + c4τ² + c5τ²ν, ν = n/1000, τ = |M|/100. Training used 20 points; 8 points at 2000 and 4000 min⁻¹ were withheld from fitting. Holdout efficiency RMSE is 0,260 percentage point. This checks an approximation of the chart, not a new measurement [4].

A loss floor of 3R25(M/Kt)² and an efficiency ceiling of 96% are used. Estimated digitization uncertainty is ±3 N·m and ±50 min⁻¹; map test conditions are not fully specified. The voltage and cooling of the tabulated MV/LC version do not establish that the chart used identical conditions. Results outside the digitized branches and in generating operation are marked as extrapolation; time spent in these conditions is accumulated.

Symmetry of losses under torque reversal is assumed only for preliminary generating calculations. At Pмех < 0, Pел = Pмех + L; regenerative efficiency is therefore not calculated by dividing by motoring efficiency. An imported map is not mirrored automatically: generating operation requires negative-torque data.

The supplied Brogen CSV files contain contour points: Eff_… headers specify efficiency, Loss_… headers specify losses in watts, and cells specify power in kW. Torque coordinates are obtained from M = 1000PкВт/(πn/30), only for n > 0. Empty and zero boundary points were not used as loss measurements. A trial fit produced unacceptable contour consistency; the dataset is retained with the conclusion rejected-for-default-use. This does not prove a manufacturer error: digitization, domain and map conditions need checking [6].

No external numerical maps have been imported. Only explicitly described built-in models are active.

4. Component selection and limits

The catalogue distinguishes EMRAX 208, 228, 268-C and 348, using only medium-voltage (MV), liquid-cooled (LC) versions. For the 228 MV/LC: 124 kW short-time S2 30 s, 68 kW continuous S1, 220/118 N·m, and 630 V for peak power. The 75 kW figure on the cover is not assigned to the LC version: it refers to different cooling. Cover and table values for the 348 conflict; the code retains the page 2 table and a warning [4,5]. The supplied v1.7 datasheets warn of a transition to a new generation; this example does not establish current availability for purchase.

Torque is constrained simultaneously by Tпаспорт, Kt·Iфаза,RMS and Pпаспорт/ω. The constant-torque/constant-power envelope is an approximate upper bound. Voltage limits, field weakening, actual inverter capability and temperature may reduce available torque. Battery DC current is determined separately and is not equated with RMS phase current.

No numerically identified map is available in this release for the 208, 268-C or 348. An explicitly labeled assumption of η = 0,92 is used with a copper-loss floor. EMRAX 228 coefficients are not scaled to other motors. A rated “up to 96%” is not constant efficiency at every operating point.

Continuous limits are used in the mission. The peak characteristic is only a short-time S2 30 s envelope: it is not accumulated into unlimited operating time and does not imply that a thermal calculation has been completed. Warm-up, cooling, repeated peaks and derating are not modeled.

BEV and series layouts use one prescribed transmission ratio. P2/combustion-engine layouts use an assumed five-speed gearbox with ratios 3,5; 2,1; 1,4; 1,0; 0,8 and a separate final drive. Gear selection considers available force and proximity to the assumed operating speed. This is not a production shift map. Inertial gear-change transients are omitted. Multiple identical electric machines are represented at a common speed and transmission ratio; independent wheel-torque distribution is not calculated.

In P2, the combustion engine disconnects below its stable operating speed and the electric machine provides launch. The comparison combustion-engine layout assumes idealized low-speed clutch slip with separate accounting for heat losses. Without the required data, clutch life and exact acceleration time are not calculated. During braking, the engine in the comparison layout is disconnected from the wheels; with positive auxiliary demand, it runs at minimum speed to supply that demand. Fuel cut-off and discharge of the low-voltage battery are not modeled in this simplification.

5. Traction and energy calculation

The required force is F = mδa + mgfcosβ + ρCdA(v + vвітру)|v + vвітру|/2 + mgsinβ, where β = arctan(grade/100). Wheel power is Pк = Fv. All internal quantities use SI units; the interface speed in km/h is converted by dividing by 3,6, and rotational speed in min⁻¹ by multiplying by π/30. Positive wind speed represents a headwind [3,9].

For traction, Pвал = Pк/ηтр; for braking, electric-machine shaft power is Pвал = Pк·ηтр. Gearbox losses are nonnegative in both directions. Available traction force is limited by μ·zвед·mgcosβ. Total braking is limited by μ·mgcosβ; regenerative braking is additionally limited by driven-axle loading. This is a simple estimate, not an ABS/ESP or load-transfer model.

Parallel layout: Pк = ηтр(PДВЗ + Pм), only for positive total power flow. Series layout: Pген,DC = ηгенPДВЗ, Pбат = Pм,DC + Pдоп − Pген,DC. The battery may assist the generator or accept its surplus within the permitted limits. When the upper SOC limit restricts charging, generator power is reduced; surplus energy does not disappear from the balance.

Actual battery flow satisfies Rint: Uк = Uoc − IR, Pбат = UкI, I = 2Pбат/[Uoc + sqrt(Uoc² − 4RPбат)]. Positive I means discharge. Current, terminal voltage, discriminant, SOC and the charge available during a step are constrained. SOCнов = SOCстар − IΔt/(3600CА·h). For R = 0, the same stable expression is used without division by R [3].

Constant Voc and R, with CА·год = 1000EкВт·год/Voc, define an assumed equivalent battery. I²R losses and the change in chemical energy UocI are accounted for separately from terminal energy. There is no Voc(SOC,T) map, dynamic polarization, aging, individual-cell voltage model or thermal validation. A displayed voltage below the motor's rated reference is explicitly flagged rather than hidden.

Braking demand is divided between permitted regeneration and friction braking. Energy charged from the engine in P2 is not counted as regeneration from vehicle motion. At high SOC or zero permitted charging current, the friction-braking share increases [9].

The hybrid controller is illustrative: SOC hysteresis of ±0,02 around the target and a wheel-power threshold. Fuel and electricity are shown separately. Lower petrol consumption while discharging the battery does not imply higher overall efficiency. A valid HEV comparison requires equal final battery energy or a documented SOC correction; no automatic “equivalent fuel” correction is invented here.

6. Scenario results and charts

The initial example is an author-defined 600 s cycle with 18 reference points, not WLTP, FTP75 or an EPA certification cycle. Speed is interpolated linearly; power flows are integrated with an internal step no greater than 1 s. A custom CSV uses columns time_s,speed_kmh,grade_pct. Time must increase and speed must be nonnegative.

First check mission completion, energy deficit, time outside the approximation domain and SOC change. Only then analyze specific consumption. If the mission is incomplete, the reported consumption sums the loads actually served at the prescribed speed; it is not a prediction of real driving range.

The power-selection chart shows wheel demand on the specified gradient and available continuous/peak power at initial SOC. These are instantaneous envelopes under current electrical and mechanical limits, not proof that peak power can be sustained along the entire route.

A range estimate is available only for BEV: constant speed on a level road, current auxiliary demand and available energy from initial SOC to the lower limit. Gradient and acceleration from the calculation point are not carried into this mode. The estimate does not account for battery temperature changes, variable speed or aging; it is not a rated driving range.

7. Checks, reproducibility and further data

Automated tests check rpm/rad·s⁻¹ conversion, map nodes and gaps, rejection of imported-map extrapolation, mechanical and DC balance, losses Rint, SOC limits, regeneration with a full battery, failure under insufficient power, preservation of unknown fuel consumption, and separation of training and verification groups.

Passing these tests confirms implementation of the stated mathematical model, not agreement with any production vehicle. Physical validation requires matched operating conditions and measurements of torque, speed, fuel flow, DC voltage/current, temperatures and final SOC.

Source files are organized by purpose: powertrain/manuscript contains small authored fragments; powertrain/src contains pure models and publication code; powertrain/data contains sourced data; powertrain/scripts reproduces fitting; tests contains checks. index.html, build/* and native JSON are generated; manually editing these derived files does not change the authored sources.

A saved powertrain-workspace/1 contains parameters, the cycle and explicitly imported maps. Results are recalculated when it is opened. Editing a field marks the result stale and blocks export until recalculation succeeds. Native engdoc exports text, scalars and explicit numerical snapshots; the Text_work editor does not independently run the vehicle integrator.

The next evidence-based step is to import an actual numerical EPA package for the selected engine/electric drive, verifying its system boundary, together with independently measured operating conditions and a cycle. Full 1ZR calibration requires the full-load curve, part-load conditions, fuel flow and indicator data; two rated maxima are not a substitute.

For further OBD calibration, each channel needs a unit, a PID/logger-calculation source, time and a validity status. Split data by trips, not adjacent rows. Do not treat MAF/lambda, Fuel Rate and Engine Power as independent channels until their definitions are established. The battery needs Udc, Idc, temperatures and traction-battery SOC specifically; regenerative charging must be distinguished from engine charging. Without these channels, results remain estimates/diagnostics rather than demonstrated parameter identification.

8. Requirements, forward acceleration and selection advisor

Forward acceleration integrates m·δ·dv/dt = Fдоступна − Fкочення − Fповітря − Fухилу. Traction is constrained by torque, power, speed, phase/DC current, battery and grip. At zero speed, torque is possible without mechanical power; copper losses remain.

Peak operation is allowed only for the shorter of the user-entered duration and the rated S2 duration. S1 is used afterward. This is a bounded single acceleration event, not a thermal model of repeated accelerations. Gear shifts are instantaneous; load transfer and detailed tire dynamics are not modeled.

Checks cover acceleration, achievable steady maximum speed at initial SOC, the specified gradient over the specified duration and, when required, electric range at constant speed. The latter is a preliminary estimate with constant Voc/R, not a WLTP result. Mission completion is checked separately.

The advisor searches a finite grid of up to 48 motor–transmission–battery configurations for the selected architecture and unchanged combustion engine. Configurations meeting both requirements and mission conditions rank first, followed by smaller assumed battery capacity and fewer motors. This is a transparent compactness rule, not cost optimization or production-component selection.

Assumptions for Rint-battery and inverter boundaries are explicitly identified. Operating points outside the map and generating assumptions remain warnings. Passing model selection does not establish experimental suitability.

9. Numerical maps and independent-trip checks

A further attempt was made to obtain EPA numerical ZIP packages; file transfer failed in this environment. A methodology link is not labeled as an imported map. The package contains controlled importers for normalized CSV/JSON and a failed-download log; synthetic tests are not described as EPA data.

An imported map must identify the component, units, component boundary, source and reference conditions. Fuel maps are not transferred between engine codes; loss maps are not transferred between motors. A missing cell is not zero fuel consumption. Interpolation outside the rectangular domain or across a gap is rejected.

For the independent OBD experiment, a separate time-series check is prepared with trip splits, units and signal provenance. Raw trips are absent here; no new GG2W training or independent validation was performed. Format examples used in tests are synthetic and are not mixed with the user's measurements.

Calibration of the 720° cycle, Voc(SOC,T), R(SOC,T), thermal limits and production hybrid controller remains separate work requiring suitable data. Existing OBD coefficients are retained unchanged.
